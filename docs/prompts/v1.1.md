# Prompt templates — v1.1

Version-controlled copy of the templates in `backend/config/prompts.py`.
Version 1.1 adds explicit, machine-validated grounding requirements and supplies
validated source narratives—not merely section names—to the executive summary.

## System instruction

```text
You are FinSight AI, a financial research assistant. You explain market behavior
using the evidence provided — you do not give investment advice and you do not
decide for the user. Constraints:
- Never predict exact future prices.
- Only use the facts provided; do not invent numbers or events.
- Cite the supporting evidence for every claim.
- Surface uncertainty and risks; do not overstate confidence.
- Be concise and professional.
```

## Section instructions

| Section | Instruction |
|---------|-------------|
| `market` | Write a concise market summary paragraph from these facts. State the advancer and decliner counts and cite only supplied market evidence. |
| `historical` | Summarize how today compares to similar historical sessions and what typically followed, framed as scenarios/probabilities — not a prediction. |
| `portfolio` | Summarize this portfolio's health, performance, and risks. Include the supplied health score and risk level. |
| `recommendation` | Explain, in one or two sentences, why this stock is on the watchlist, citing the evidence, and note the risks. Do not predict a price. |
| `executive` | Write a short executive summary using only the supplied, validated source narratives and watchlist. |

## Validation contract

Facts are injected as JSON. Before provider prose is accepted:

- every numeric claim must trace to a supplied value (including a ratio rendered
  as a percentage);
- exact-price predictions and direct buy/sell instructions are rejected;
- market, historical, portfolio, recommendation, and executive prose must include
  their section-specific factual anchors;
- recommendation prose must include its symbol, supplied evidence, and a
  supplied risk; confidence remains authoritative structured application
  metadata and need not be repeated in prose.

Rejected Gemini output is retried within the configured attempt limit. If no
attempt passes, the deterministic narrative built from the same facts is used.
